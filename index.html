Little lessons

Cook with confidence.

Good Indian cooking is all about patience, aroma, and tasting as you go.

01

Bloom your spices

Warm whole spices in oil until fragrant before adding anything else.

02

Keep ginger and garlic close

A spoonful of the fresh paste is the quickest route to deep flavor.

03

Finish with brightness

A squeeze of lime or a handful of cilantro wakes up a rich curry.
